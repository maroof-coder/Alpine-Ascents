02 / History

A tradition
written in stone.

More than two centuries of exploration. Mountaineering evolved from early exploration into a combination of adventure, science, technical skill and responsible environmental travel.

1786

The Birth of Modern Alpinism

The successful ascent of Mont Blanc became one of the defining moments in the history of European mountaineering.

Annapurna became the first mountain above 8,000 metres to be successfully climbed.

Mount Everest was successfully climbed, creating one of the most famous achievements in exploration history.

The Rise of Technical Climbing

Improved equipment and climbing methods opened new possibilities on increasingly difficult routes.

04 / Mountaineering

Learn the
mountain language.

Mountaineering includes alpine climbing, expedition climbing, rock climbing, ice climbing and mixed climbing. Each style requires different levels of preparation.

Climbers use route planning, navigation, rope systems, climbing equipment and environmental awareness to travel through mountain terrain.

Mountain shelters can include tents, huts and established camps designed to provide protection from changing weather.

Mountain environments can involve rapidly changing weather, difficult terrain and altitude. Proper planning is essential.

11 / Guidelines

Respect the
mountain code.

01

Plan Ahead

Understand the route, weather conditions, equipment requirements and experience level.

02

Respect Nature

Protect mountain ecosystems, minimize waste and leave natural areas as you found them.

03

Know Your Limits

Recognize changing conditions and personal limitations.

04

Stay Together

Good communication and teamwork help groups respond effectively.

05

Check Conditions

Mountain weather can change rapidly. Always consider current conditions.

06

Leave No Trace

Carry waste back, avoid damaging vegetation and respect wildlife.
